Common questions
I can't open a quest box
Quest boxes can only be opened when you are physically within 150 metres of them (300 metres with Lacigam Premium). Check that Location is enabled for Lacigam in iOS Settings, then tap SCAN on the map to refresh the boxes near you.

No boxes are appearing
Boxes are generated around wherever you are. Open the Map tab and tap SCAN. If none appear, make sure you have granted location access and have a working internet connection.

I bought Premium but it isn't showing
Open your Profile, tap the Premium banner, and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If it still doesn't unlock, email us.

I forgot my password
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your username. A reset link will be sent to the email address on your account.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including your photos and your sign-in record. It cannot be undone.

My quest is "pending review"
Quest photos are reviewed by our moderators. Your XP is awarded immediately — you don't need to wait. If a photo is rejected, the XP for that quest is removed and you'll see the reason in your journal.
